1Concept

Management = process of getting things done to achieve goals effectively & efficiently. Koontz: "Designing and maintaining an environment in which individuals, working together in groups, efficiently accomplish selected aims."
⚙️ ProcessThe primary functions management performs to get things done: Planning, Organising, Staffing, Directing, Controlling.
🎯 EffectivenessDoing the RIGHT task. Focus = end result / goal achieved.
💰 EfficiencyDoing the task RIGHT. Focus = minimum cost (input vs output).
🔑 Effective = End result · Efficient = Economical

Case (Target: 500 units @ ₹100 = ₹50,000)

  • Ashita: 500 units for ₹55,000 → Effective ✓ · Efficient ✗ (target met, cost too high)
  • Lakshita: 450 units @ ₹90 → Efficient ✓ · Effective ✗ (cheap, target missed)
Ideal = both. Poor management = neither.

2Characteristics (7)

🧠 Good Apples Make Cool Green Drinks Instantly
  • Goal-oriented — unites everyone's effort towards organisation's goals
  • All-pervasive — needed everywhere (school, hospital, petrol pump; India or USA). WHAT is same, HOW differs (culture)
  • Multidimensional — 3 dimensions: Work · People (individuals + groups) · Operations (input → output)
  • Continuous — functions done simultaneously, all the time
  • Group activity — people with different needs work towards one common goal
  • Dynamic — adapts to environment (McDonald's changed menu for India)
  • Intangible — can't be seen, only felt (targets met, happy staff, order not chaos)

3Objectives (3)

🧠 Organisational · Social · Personal → Org ladder: Survival → Profit → Growth
  • Organisational — Survival (revenue ≥ costs) · Profit (covers costs & risks, incentive) · Growth (indicators: sales, no. of employees, no. of products, capital investment)
  • Social — benefit society: eco-friendly production, jobs for underprivileged, schools & healthcare (e.g. ITC e-Choupal)
  • Personal — employees' needs: financial (salary), social (recognition), higher (growth). Must match with org goals
📌 Indian Railways solar train → Social (green) + Organisational (₹12 lakh saving = profit)

4Importance (5)

🧠 Good Employees Develop People & Society
  • Group goals — gives common direction to individual efforts
  • Efficiency ↑ — reduces cost, increases productivity
  • Dynamic organisation — helps people adapt to change (people resist change)
  • Personal objectives — through motivation & leadership
  • Society's development — quality products, jobs, new technology

5Art · Science · Profession

✓ Met · ◐ Partly met · ✗ Not met  |  Needs what the feature requires · Mgmt how management fits

🎨 Management as an Art

Art = skilful & personal application of existing knowledge to achieve desired results. Acquired through study, observation & experience.
🧠 Talented People Practise → Theory · Personalised · Practice
  • 1. Theoretical knowledge ✓
    NeedsBasic principles laid down by experts (books on dance, music, acting)
    MgmtVast literature in marketing, finance, HR; manager learns through study, observation & experience
  • 2. Personalised application ✓
    NeedsUse of knowledge differs person to person (two dancers always differ)
    MgmtEach manager applies the same principles in own way to suit the situation → different management styles
  • 3. Practice & creativity ✓
    NeedsCreative practice of theory (same 7 notes → unique music)
    MgmtManager uses creativity, imagination, initiative & innovation; perfection comes after long practice
✅ Fully an ART. A good manager stands out by ability to put principles into practice

🔬 Management as a Science

Science = systematised body of knowledge explaining general truths / general laws.
🧠 Scientists Experiment Universally → Systematised · Experiments · Universal
  • 1. Systematised knowledge ✓
    NeedsOrganised knowledge based on cause & effect (apple falls → gravity)
    MgmtOwn theories, principles & vocabulary (like cricket terms); also draws from Economics, Sociology, Psychology, Maths
  • 2. Based on experimentation ◐
    NeedsPrinciples developed by observation, tested by repeated experiments under controlled conditions
    MgmtPrinciples evolved through experiments in organisations (Taylor, Fayol) BUT deals with humans → results can't be exactly predicted or repeated
  • 3. Universal validity ✗
    NeedsPrinciples apply everywhere, every time
    MgmtPrinciples must be modified for each situation; still give standard techniques & are used to train managers
◐ INEXACT science (because human behaviour is unpredictable)
🔑 Principles = Science · Practice = Art → not mutually exclusive, they complement each other

👔 Management as a Profession

🧠 Big Restaurants Always Earn Stars → Body · Restricted · Association · Ethics · Service
  • 1. Well-defined body of knowledge ✓
    NeedsSpecialised knowledge acquired through instruction
    MgmtTaught at colleges & IIMs, plus books & journals; entry to institutes via exams
  • 2. Restricted entry ✗
    NeedsEntry only through exam/degree (CA must clear ICAI exam)
    MgmtAnyone can be a manager; no degree compulsory anywhere in the world (only desirable)
  • 3. Professional association ◐
    NeedsBody that regulates entry & grants certificate to practise (lawyers → Bar Council)
    MgmtAIMA exists, but membership is optional & it has no statutory (legal) backing
  • 4. Ethical code of conduct ◐
    NeedsBinding code guiding members (doctors take an oath)
    MgmtAIMA has a code of conduct, but managers aren't forced to follow it
  • 5. Service motive ◐
    NeedsDedicated service to clients (lawyer ensures client gets justice)
    MgmtMain aim = org goals (profit / service); but efficient mgmt serves society automatically via quality products at fair prices
◐ NOT a full-fledged profession, but professional in character to a large extent

6Levels of Management

TOP · CEO, Chairman, President, VP, CFO
Set goals & strategies · analyse environment · coordinate all depts · responsible for survival & social impact
MIDDLE · Division heads (e.g. Production Mgr)
Link between top & lower · interpret policies · ensure staff · assign duties · motivate · cooperate with other depts
OPERATIONAL · Foremen, Supervisors
Oversee workers · pass instructions · maintain quality · minimise wastage · ensure safety
🔑 Top Thinks · Middle Mediates · Operational Oversees

7Functions (5)

🧠 Please Organise Some Delicious Cake
Planning
Decide in advance what & who. Predicts problems (can't prevent them)
Organising
Assign duties, group tasks, set authority, allocate resources
Staffing
Right person, right job: recruit, select, place, train
Directing
Lead, motivate, communicate, supervise
Controlling
Set standards → measure → compare → correct
Functions are interrelated, not done strictly one after another.

8Coordination

Synchronising the activities of different departments towards a common goal. The ESSENCE of management, like the thread in a garland. NOT a separate function.

Features (6)

🧠 I CARD U
  • Integrates group efforts — diverse interests → one purpose
  • Continuous — from planning to controlling
  • All-pervasive — all levels & all departments
  • Responsibility of all managers — top, middle & operational
  • Deliberate — conscious effort, doesn't happen by itself
  • Unity of action — binding force between departments
Cooperation without coordination= wasted effort
Coordination without cooperation= employee dissatisfaction

Why needed (3)

🧠 Get Food Soon
  • Growth in size — more people → must match individual & org goals
  • Functional differentiation — depts clash (marketing wants discounts, finance refuses)
  • Specialisation — specialists ignore others' advice → need a neutral coordinator
