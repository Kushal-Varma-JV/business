1Concept

Business environment = sum total of all individuals, institutions & other forces that are outside the control of a business but may affect its performance. "Just take the universe, subtract from it the subset that represents the organisation, and the remainder is environment."
👥 Individuals & institutionsConsumers, competitors, government, consumer groups, courts, media
🌐 ForcesEconomic, social, political, technological, legal

How outside forces hit a business

💸 Taxes ↑Things become expensive to buy
💻 New technologyExisting products become obsolete
🏛️ Political uncertaintyFear in investors' minds
👗 Fashion & taste changeDemand shifts to new products
⚔️ Competition ↑Profit margins fall
🔑 A firm can't control its environment → it can only respond & adapt
📌 Dharamveer Kamboj (rickshaw puller → multipurpose fruit processing machine) → social, economic & technological impact on food processing + better quality of life

2Features (7)

🧠 Ten Small Insects Danced Under Colourful Rainbows
  • Totality of external forces — sum total of everything outside the firm; aggregative in nature
  • Specific & general forces — Specific: affect a firm directly & immediately · General: affect all firms, so one firm only indirectly
  • Inter-relatedness — elements are linked: life expectancy & health awareness ↑ → demand for health products (fat-free oil, health resorts) → lifestyles change
  • Dynamic — keeps changing: technology, consumer preferences, new competition
  • Uncertainty — future hard to predict, especially in IT & fashion
  • Complexity — easy to understand in parts, hard as a whole (hard to tell which factor changed demand)
  • Relativity — differs by country & region: sarees in demand in India, almost none in France; politics in USA ≠ China

⚖️ Specific vs General Forces

BasisSpecificGeneral
EffectDirect & immediateIndirect
ScopeIndividual firms, day-to-day workingAll business enterprises

🔑 The 5 dimensions (economic, social…) = general environment

3Importance (6)

🧠 Only The Tall Children Play Polo
  • Opportunities & first mover advantage — opportunities = positive external trends; spot them early, exploit before competitors (Maruti: small cars, rising petrol prices + big middle class)
  • Threats & early warning signals — threats = external trends that hinder; MNC entering with substitutes → improve quality, cut cost, aggressive ads
  • Tapping useful resources — get inputs from environment, return outputs it wants
  • Coping with rapid changes — the PACE of change matters: turbulent markets, less brand loyalty, fragmented markets, demanding customers, global competition
  • Planning & policy formulation — basis for future course of action (planning) & decision guidelines (policy); new players → rethink strategy
  • Improving performance — firms that keep monitoring the environment improve now & succeed longer
⬅️ Inputs from environmentFinance, machines, raw material, power, water, labour (from financiers, govt, suppliers)
➡️ Outputs to environmentGoods & services to customers, taxes to govt, returns to investors

6New Industrial Policy (July 1991)

🧠 Features (6): Lazy Pandas Don't Fetch Apples Fast
  • Licensing — compulsory licensing reduced to 6 industries
  • Public sector — many industries dereserved; limited to 4 strategic industries
  • Disinvestment — in many public sector enterprises
  • Foreign capital — foreign equity share raised; 100% FDI allowed in many activities
  • Automatic permission — for technology agreements with foreign companies
  • FIPB set up — Foreign Investment Promotion Board to promote & channelise foreign investment
➕ Also → obstacles to large industrial houses removed · small-scale sector assured all help
🔑 In essence → free industry from licensing = Liberalisation · cut public sector role = Privatisation · invite foreign participation = Globalisation

7Liberalisation · Privatisation · Globalisation

🔓 Liberalisation

Freeing Indian business & industry from all unnecessary controls & restrictions. Signalled the end of the licence-permit-quota raj.
🧠 (7) Little Sparrows Make Pretty Twig Igloos Fast
  • Licensing abolished — in most industries except a short list
  • Scale freedom — no restriction on expansion or contraction
  • Movement of goods & services — restrictions removed
  • Prices — freedom to fix prices of goods & services
  • Tax rates reduced — unnecessary controls lifted
  • Import/export procedures simplified
  • Foreign capital & technology — easier to attract

🏭 Privatisation

Greater role to the private sector & reduced role to the public sector in nation building. A reversal of the earlier development strategy.
🧠 Steps (3): Ram Plays Rugby
  • Redefined role of public sector — in New Industrial Policy 1991
  • Planned disinvestment — of public sector enterprises
  • Referred sick units — loss-making PSUs sent to BIFR (Board of Industrial & Financial Reconstruction)
📤 DisinvestmentTransfer of PSUs to private sector → govt stake diluted
🔑 51% ruleGovt stake falls below 51% → ownership & management pass to private sector

🌍 Globalisation

Integration of world economies into a cohesive global economy; more interaction & interdependence among nations.
⛔ Before 1991Imports regulated via licensing, tariff restrictions, quantitative restrictions
✅ After 1991Import liberalisation, export promotion via tariff rationalisation, forex reforms
🚀 Made possible byFast technology + liberal trade policies → distance & borders no longer barriers
🧠 Truly global economy (6): Good Cats Ignore Pesky Dogs Gracefully
  • Goods & services — flow freely across nations
  • Capital — flows freely
  • Information & technology — flow freely
  • People — move freely across borders
  • Dispute settlement — common acceptable mechanism
  • Global governance perspective

9Demonetisation

Nov 8, 2016: ₹500 & ₹1,000 notes stopped being legal tender (except specified uses like utility bills). 86% of currency in circulation became invalid; deposited in banks with withdrawal limits. RBI then issued new ₹500 & ₹2,000 notes.

Aims (4)

🧠 Cats Can't Ignore Bones
  • Corruption curbed
  • Counterfeiting curbed
  • Illegal activities — stop use of high-value notes for them
  • Black money — income not declared to tax authorities

Features (4)

🧠 Tall Trees Shade Lakes
  • Tax administration measure — declared cash deposited easily; black money holders declare & pay tax at penalty rate
  • Tax evasion won't be tolerated — signals a shift in govt's stance
  • Savings channelled into formal system — new deposits give banks a base for loans at lower interest
  • Less-cash / cash-lite economy — more digital payments, better tax compliance ✗ needs phones, PoS machines, internet
